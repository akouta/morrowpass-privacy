Improve a card scan

  1. Place the card on a contrasting, evenly lit surface.
  2. Keep all four corners inside the frame.
  3. Avoid glare, shadows, and motion blur.
  4. Rotate or refine the crop if the card is sideways or an edge is incorrect.
  5. Retake or import a sharper photo if key text remains unreadable.
  6. If the front refers to the reverse or misses useful details, follow the optional back-side prompt; otherwise continue without it.
  7. Optionally choose familiar issuing regions in Settings → Card Regions. Worldwide remains the default and no country is excluded.
  8. Review every suggested detail and explicitly confirm the printed expiry before saving.

MorrowPass does not request the back of a payment card. Never scan a visible security code, signature, PIN, or magnetic-stripe data for troubleshooting.

Possible duplicate warning

MorrowPass compares confirmed card details with readable local records before saving. If it finds a likely match, choose Keep Reviewing to check the form. Use Save as Separate Card only when the new record is genuinely a different card, or explicitly choose the saved card that the scan replaces. A linked replacement is not treated as a duplicate of its predecessor.

MorrowPass Free and Lifetime Pro

MorrowPass Free supports up to four active cards. Archive an active card to free a slot without deleting it. Lifetime Pro removes the active-card limit with one non-consumable purchase; it is not a subscription. The United States base price is USD 9.99, and the app shows Apple's localized price for the current storefront.

Apple StoreKit handles purchase and Restore Purchases. If an earlier purchase is not recognized, confirm the same Apple Account is being used for App Store purchases, connect to the internet, open Settings → MorrowPass Pro, and tap Restore Purchases. Approval-pending purchases unlock automatically only after StoreKit verifies them.

A purchase problem never deletes or hides an existing card. MorrowPass has no purchase backend and cannot view Apple Account credentials, issue refunds, or manually create a Pro entitlement. Billing and refund requests must be handled through Apple.

The purchase is governed by Apple's Standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement.

Encrypted backup and recovery

Create or restore a password-encrypted .morrowpassbackup file from Settings. MorrowPass never stores the password and cannot recover it, so keep the password separate from the backup file. Retained images are excluded by default and require explicit export opt-in; payment-card images are never included.

Import first decrypts, authenticates, and checks the complete file. After separate confirmation, it replaces the entire current library rather than merging it, then rebuilds local reminders. A wrong password or tampered file must not change the current library.

The normal on-device encryption key is tied to the installation and does not transfer through an ordinary migration or restore. If protected data is unavailable, unlock the device and use Retry. The other recovery choices are Restore Encrypted Backup or Delete Unreadable Card.

Renewal guidance boundary

MorrowPass records your renewal plan; it does not verify issuer rules, submit renewals, or provide legal, medical, financial, or government advice. Always confirm current requirements directly with the official issuer using a link or telephone number you trust.
