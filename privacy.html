Privacy Policy

MorrowPass keeps card-expiry tracking local to your Apple device and gives you control over retained images, reminders, and backups.

Last updated: August 3, 2026

Overview

MorrowPass is a private, on-device card-expiry library. It has no account, advertising, analytics, tracking, developer backend, or integrated third-party SDK that collects user data. The MorrowPass developer does not receive card images, recognized text, saved card details, searches, reminder activity, or backup contents.

Information handled on the device

MorrowPass can handle information that you choose to scan, import, type, or approve, including:

  • front and optional back card images;
  • card category, display name, holder, issuer, country or region, masked identifier, issue date, and expiry date;
  • optional preferred card regions used to improve local interpretation;
  • reminder choices and renewal status;
  • renewal links, telephone numbers, notes, and checklist steps; and
  • bounded source evidence for approved OCR fields.

OCR and card-edge processing use Apple frameworks on the device. Unfiltered raw OCR output and transient orientation observations are not persisted. Retaining card images is optional, and retained images and sensitive card fields are encrypted.

Payment-card records are restricted to a nickname, issuer, final four digits, and expiry. MorrowPass does not persist full card numbers, PINs, CVVs, magnetic-stripe data, or payment-card images in portable backups. MorrowPass does not request the back of a payment card.

Preferred card regions are optional app settings stored on the device. They softly prioritize familiar layouts only after matching printed evidence; they do not exclude cards from another country or automatically populate a saved country or region.

Permissions

  • Camera: requested only after you choose a camera scan.
  • Photos: import uses Apple's system photo picker. MorrowPass receives only the item you select and does not request broad Photo Library access.
  • Notifications: requested only when you save with reminders enabled. Reminders are local and use non-sensitive wording by default.
  • Face ID or device passcode: used only if you enable App Lock. MorrowPass does not receive or store biometric data.

Permission choices can be changed in iOS Settings. Features that depend on a permission may stop working when it is revoked, while the rest of the local library remains available subject to App Lock.

Storage, encryption, and backups

The library is stored in MorrowPass's app container under iOS data protection. Sensitive values and optional retained images use an app-held encryption key protected by Keychain. The normal device key is tied to the installation and does not transfer through an ordinary migration or restore.

You can explicitly create a password-encrypted .morrowpassbackup file. MorrowPass never stores or receives the export password and cannot recover it. Retained images are excluded by default and require a separate opt-in; payment-card images are always excluded.

You choose the export destination. Apple or another file provider may store or synchronize that encrypted file under its own terms, but MorrowPass does not operate a backup service, upload the file to the developer, or have access to the chosen provider account. Keep the backup password separate from the file.

Import authenticates and validates a complete backup before making changes. After separate confirmation, restore replaces the current library rather than merging it and then rebuilds local reminders.

Free and Lifetime Pro purchases

MorrowPass Free supports up to four active cards. Archiving an active card frees a Free slot without deleting the archived record. A one-time, non-consumable MorrowPass Lifetime Pro purchase removes the active-card limit. It is not a subscription and has no recurring charge.

Purchase and the explicit Restore Purchases action use Apple StoreKit. The United States base price is USD 9.99, while the app displays the price and currency supplied by Apple for the current storefront. Apple processes the transaction under its terms and privacy policy. MorrowPass receives only the Apple-signed information needed to verify whether Lifetime Pro is active on the device. The developer operates no purchase server, and no card or backup content is included in StoreKit requests.

Existing saved cards remain available to view, edit, archive, export, and delete if a purchase is pending, unavailable, refunded, or not restored. Restore Purchases runs only after an explicit user action and may ask the user to authenticate with the App Store.

Sharing and third parties

MorrowPass does not sell, rent, share, or transmit user data to the developer, advertisers, data brokers, analytics providers, or issuers. It does not contact an issuer or download renewal guidance. Generic guidance templates are bundled with the app.

Apple system services, including StoreKit, and any file provider you deliberately choose are governed by their own privacy terms. MorrowPass integrates no third-party code that receives card or usage data.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you edit or delete it, remove the app, or restore a different library. A card can be archived or permanently deleted from Card Detail. Permanent deletion removes its record and associated encrypted retained data from MorrowPass.

Deletion cannot erase copies already stored in exported backup files, device backups, file-provider history, or filesystem snapshots. Those copies must be deleted separately through the system or service that stores them.

If protected data cannot be decrypted, MorrowPass hides it and excludes the record from normal detail, search, reminders, duplicate matching, and replacement. Recovery choices are Retry, Restore Encrypted Backup, or Delete Unreadable Card.

These public pages

The Privacy and Support pages are static files hosted by GitHub Pages. Opening them causes your browser to connect to GitHub, which may process ordinary web-request information under its privacy statement. These pages add no MorrowPass analytics, advertising, contact form, or tracking scripts. Opening them from MorrowPass does not send card or library data.
